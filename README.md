# 🍕 Collect Cheese Pizza

A fun, interactive browser-based game where your goal is to collect delicious cheese pizza and reach 10 TB!

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Click the "Click for Pizza!" button** or any pizza tile on the board to collect cheese pizza
2. Each pizza you collect adds 1 TB to your total
3. **Win the game** by collecting 10 TB of delicious cheese pizza 🎉
4. Watch your progress on the progress bar and stat boxes
5. Use the **Reset Game** button to start over

## 🎯 Features

- **Interactive Game Board**: A 4x4 grid of pizza tiles that appear randomly
- **Progress Tracking**: Watch your TB collection grow toward the 10 TB goal
- **Visual Feedback**: Animated pizza emojis pop up as you collect
- **Smooth Animations**: Tiles have hover and click animations for satisfying gameplay
- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop and mobile devices
- **Beautiful UI**: Gradient backgrounds and modern styling

## 📊 Game Stats

- **Total Collected**: How many TB of pizza you've accumulated
- **Goal**: The target amount (10 TB)
- **Pizzas Caught**: Total number of pizzas collected
- **Progress Bar**: Visual representation of your progress toward 10 TB

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Start clicking to collect pizza!
3. Have fun! 🍕

## 💡 Tips

- Click as fast as you can to collect pizza quickly
- Watch for pizzas appearing on the tiles
- The progress bar will show you how close you are to winning
- Challenge yourself to see how fast you can reach 10 TB!

Enjoy your cheesy pizza collection adventure! 🍕✨
